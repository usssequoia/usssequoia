Lieutenant / Petty Officer

You must meet the following requirements to be eligible for a promotion to this rank:

You continue to meet all the requirements for promotion to Lieutenant Junior Grade or Crewman (First Class)
You have attended at least one charity event held by USS Sequoia within a rolling twelve month period
  • You have hosted at least one event for USS Sequoia
  • Hosting in this case is defined as having held primary responsibility for the planning and/or execution of an event that has been publicly advertised to and attended by other members of USS Sequoia.
You have been an active member of THE FLEET for at least nine months
Your conduct within THE FLEET has been of an acceptable standard

The Commanding Officer or First Officer of USS Sequoia (Captain Isabel Roybal or Lieutenant Commander Jocelyn Cliff, respectively) will make the determination of whether a member has the required knowledge, meets conduct standards, and qualifies for promotion.
