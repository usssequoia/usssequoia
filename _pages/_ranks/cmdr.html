Commander

You must meet the following requirements to be eligible for a promotion to this rank:

You continue to meet all the requirements for promotion to Lieutenant Commander
You own a uniform in the Strange New Worlds style meeting USS Sequoia guidelines
You have passed a quiz on the contents of THE FLEET handbook
You have been an active member of THE FLEET for at least twelve months
Your conduct within THE FLEET has been of an acceptable standard

In addition, there must be an available spot within the Pacific Station regional group for someone of Commander rank

A member of THE FLEET Admiralty will make the determination of whether a member has the required knowledge, meets conduct standards, and qualifies for promotion
