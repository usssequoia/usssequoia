Ensign / Crewman (Second Class)

You must meet the following requirements to be eligible for a promotion to this rank:

  • You have chosen a department
  • Outside of Senior Staff, department choices do not imply any special skills or any volunteered duties with THE FLEET. Your selected department can be changed at any time
  • Valid departments:
  • Navigation (Red Shirt)
  • Communications (Red Shirt)
  • Operations (Red Shirt)
  • Supply (Red Shirt)
  • Engineering (Red Shirt)
  • Science (Blue Shirt)
  • Command (Gold Shirt)
  • Medical (Light Blue or White Shirt)
  • Councilor (Blue Shirt)
  • Section 31 (████████; Shirt) (Requires approval from ████████)
You have working knowledge of USS Sequoia and its activities
  • You have knowledge of other ships in the Pacific Station squadron
  • This requirement is included for consistency with other ships in THE FLEET. However, as of 1/1/2025, USS Sequoia is not in a squadron, and is the only ship attached to Pacific Station
You have general knowledge of THE FLEET
Your conduct within THE FLEET has been of an acceptable standard

The Commanding Officer or First Officer of USS Sequoia (Captain Isabel Roybal or Lieutenant Commander Jocelyn Cliff, respectively) will make the determination of whether a member has the required knowledge, meets conduct standards, and qualifies for promotion.
