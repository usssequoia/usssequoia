Lieutenant Junior Grade / Crewman (First Class)

You must meet the following requirements to be eligible for a promotion to this rank:

You continue to meet all the requirements for promotion to Ensign or Crewman (Second Class)
You have engaged the group on social media
You have attended and interacted with other members in at least one event per month for three consecutive months
Your conduct within THE FLEET has been of an acceptable standard

Your department head (Senior Staff of USS Sequoia in a matching department), or the Commanding Officer or First Officer of USS Sequoia (Captain Isabel Roybal or Lieutenant Commander Jocelyn Cliff, respectively) will make the determination of whether a member has the required knowledge, meets conduct standards, and qualifies for promotion.
