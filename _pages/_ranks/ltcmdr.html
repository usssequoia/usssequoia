Lieutenant Commander / Chief Petty Officer

You must meet the following requirements to be eligible for a promotion to this rank:

You continue to meet all the requirements for promotion to Lieutenant or Petty Officer
You have accepted assigned responsibilities within USS Sequoia
You have fulfilled your responsibilities at a satisfactory quality level and delivered at agreed-upon deadlines
You have been an active member of THE FLEET for at least twelve months
Your conduct within THE FLEET has been of an acceptable standard

The Commanding Officer of USS Sequoia (Captain Isabel Roybal) will make the determination of whether a member has the required knowledge, meets conduct standards, and qualifies for promotion.
