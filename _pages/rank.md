Ranks exist in <span style="white-space: no-wrap">USS Sequoia</span> and <span style="white-space: no-wrap">THE FLEET</span> as a way to recognize accomplishment, long-term dedication, and good conduct within the organization. However, they confer no special privileges within the organization. Rank may be revoked or reduced in response to poor conduct or inactivity. Members have the right to know (either verbally or in writing) if they will be demoted in rank and for what reason. This can be done by the <span style="white-space: no-wrap">Commanding Officer</span> or <span style="white-space: no-wrap">First Officer</span> (<span style="white-space: no-wrap">Captain Isabel Roybal</span> or <span style="white-space: no-wrap">Lieutenant Commander Jocelyn Cliff</span>, respectively), who will meet and discuss the situation with the given crew member.

# Civilian Designations and Automatic Ranks

## 21st Century Citizen
Any person who interacts with <span style="white-space: no-wrap">THE FLEET</span> or <span style="white-space: no-wrap">USS Sequoia</span> in any way (including by joining the Facebook group or signing up for the mailing list) attains this designation by default.

## Fleet Guest
If you do not wish to pursue promotion, or otherwise don't want to identify as a participating member of <span style="white-space: no-wrap">THE FLEET</span> or <span style="white-space: no-wrap">USS Sequoia</span> for any reason, you may request to be referred to as a <span style="white-space: no-wrap">"Fleet Guest".</span>

## Crewman (Third Class)
Any member over the age of 15 who has either attended any <span style="white-space: no-wrap">USS Sequoia</span> event (online or in person) or has joined the Discord will automatically qualify for this rank.

## Junior Cadet Ranks
Any member who would qualify for a rank of <span style="white-space: no-wrap">Crewman (Third Class)</span> or higher but is under the age of 15 will instead be given a <span style="white-space: no-wrap">Junior Cadet</span> rank.
* Any member under the age of 12 will be given a rank of <span style="white-space: no-wrap">Junior Cadet (1st Year).</span>
* Any member over the age of 12 but under the age of 13 will be given a rank of <span style="white-space: no-wrap">Junior Cadet (2nd Year).</span>
* Any member over the age of 13 but under the age of 14 will be given a rank of <span style="white-space: no-wrap">Junior Cadet (3rd Year).</span>
* Any member over the age of 14 but under the age of 15 will be given a rank of <span style="white-space: no-wrap">Junior Cadet (4th Year).</span>

## Ranks from other ships in THE FLEET:
A member who has already been promoted up to <span style="white-space: no-wrap">Lieutenant Junior Grade</span> on another ship in <span style="white-space: no-wrap">THE FLEET</span> will retain that rank upon transfer to <span style="white-space: no-wrap">USS Sequoia</span>. Members with higher ranks than that may only retain their previous rank if they've held it for at least six months prior to transfer. Founding members of <span style="white-space: no-wrap">USS Sequoia</span> are exempted from this requirement. If there are fewer than five <span style="white-space: no-wrap">Senior Officers on <span style="white-space: no-wrap">USS Sequoia</span>, and a member transfers with the intent to become a <span style="white-space: no-wrap">Senior Officer</span>, they may also be exempted from this requirement, at the <span style="white-space: no-wrap">Commanding Officer</span> or Admiralty's discretion.

# Merit-based Ranks

*Promotion beyond any of the above automatic ranks depends on whether you own a uniform in the <span style="white-space: no-wrap">Strange New Worlds</span> style that meets <span style="white-space: no-wrap">USS Sequoia</span> guidelines. Members without a uniform will be promoted along the Enlisted track:*

Crewman (Third Class) > Crewman (Second Class) > Crewman (First Class) > Petty Officer > Chief Petty Officer

*Members with a uniform will be promoted along the Officer track:*

Crewman (Third Class) > Ensign > Lieutenant Junior Grade > Lieutenant > Lieutenant Commander > Commander > Captain

*If you have already been promoted along the <span style="white-space: no-wrap">Enlisted Track</span> but then acquire a qualifying uniform afterwards, you will be immediately qualified for promotion to the equivalent rank in the <span style="white-space: no-wrap">Officer Track.</span>*
